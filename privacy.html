Under the Tree

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Under the Tree is a Christmas wishlist app for families and friend groups. This page says what the app saves, where it goes, and who can see it. It covers the website, the iPhone and iPad app, and the Apple Watch app.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • No accounts. The app never asks for an email, a phone number, your location or your contacts.
  • What you add to a group can be seen by the people in that group, which means anyone who has its invite code.
  • You can delete everything of yours from inside the app at any time.

What the app saves

Only what people type or pick in the app:

  • The name of the group, and the names people use in it (first names or nicknames are all it needs).
  • Wishes: what it is, a price, notes, web links and photos you choose to add.
  • Picks (what you plan to give), Secret Santa votes and who drew who, advent doors you opened, and game scores.
  • Your answers to the favorites quiz (favorite candy, sizes and so on), if you fill it out.
  • Your ornament, and a profile picture if you add one.
  • A random ID for each device, so the group knows which phone or watch picked which name. It is not tied to who you are.
  • A PIN or password, if you choose to set one. It is saved scrambled, never as the real thing.
  • A note that says you have the app open right now (the green dot), unless you turn that off.
  • Reports, if someone reports a wish.

Where it is kept

Group data is stored with Google Firebase, a database service run by Google, on servers in the United States. A copy is also kept on your own device so the app opens quickly and works without a connection.

Who can see it

People in your group. Anyone with a group's invite code can join it and see what is in it, so only share the code with people you want in the group.

Inside a group the app keeps some things to yourself: your picks, who you voted for, who you got in Secret Santa, and which advent doors you opened. Those are still saved with the group's data, so please do not use the app to store real secrets.

We can look at a group's data only when we need to, for example to act on a report or to fix a problem someone asked us about.

Other services the app talks to

Like any website, these services see the internet address of your device when the app loads something from them:

  • Google Firebase, for saving and syncing the group.
  • Google Fonts, for the lettering.
  • Unsplash, for the background photos.
  • incompetech.com, for the Christmas music (by Kevin MacLeod).
  • Any shop you open by tapping a link on a wish.

Children

Families use this app together. If you are under 13, use it only with a parent or guardian who has said yes and set up the group. The app does not ask children for personal information. Please do not add full names, home addresses, school names or anything else private to a wish or a name.

A parent or guardian can delete a child's data in the app (see below) or ask us to do it.

Deleting your data

  • Your own things: open the menu, tap Safety and rules, then Delete my stuff. That removes your wishes, photos, picks, quiz answers, scores, votes, picture and ornament from the group for everyone. You can take your name off too.
  • One wish: open it on your own list and take it off.
  • A whole group: ask us (contact below) and include the group's invite code.

Leaving a group on your device does not delete what you added. Use Delete my stuff first if you want it gone.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. We may remove groups that nobody has opened for a long time.
