Under the Tree

Terms of Use

Last updated November 18, 2026

Under the Tree is a free Christmas wishlist app for families and friend groups. By starting or joining a group you agree to these terms.

Who can use it

Anyone 13 or older. If you are younger than 13, use it only with a parent or guardian who has said yes and set up the group.

The rules for what you add

Wishes, names, photos and anything else you add can be seen by the other people in your group. Keep it kind and keep it family friendly. Do not add anything that is:

  • mean, bullying, hateful or threatening;
  • sexual, violent or otherwise not okay for kids;
  • against the law, or a link to something that is;
  • someone's private information, such as a home address or a photo they would not want shared;
  • spam, or pretending to be another person.

There is no tolerance for this kind of content or for people who abuse others in a group.

Reporting and removing

  • Anyone in a group can report or hide a wish, and hide a person's whole list, from the wish itself.
  • Whoever runs a group sees its reports and can take a wish or a person off the group.
  • You can also report a problem to us from the help page. We look at reports within 24 hours and may remove content, people or whole groups that break these rules.

Your content

What you add stays yours. You let the app store it and show it to the people in your group, which is the whole point of the app. You can delete it at any time (menu, Safety and rules, Delete my stuff).

Invite codes

Anyone who has a group's invite code can join it and see what is in it. Share the code only with people you want in the group.

No promises

The app is free and provided as it is. We work to keep it running and to keep your lists safe, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of mistakes, and we are not responsible for gifts bought, not bought or bought twice. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.
