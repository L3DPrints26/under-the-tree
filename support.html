Help

Quick answers first. If you still need us, how to reach us is at the bottom.

Getting started

  • Start a group: open the app, pick Family or Friends, and give it a name. You get an invite code.
  • Invite people: open the menu and tap Invite people. Send them the code or the link.
  • Join a group: tap "I have a code to join" and type the code someone sent you.
  • Pick your name: tap your name the first time you open the group. It is then yours on that phone.

Apple Watch

  • Open the app on the watch and type your group's invite code.
  • Pick your name. If your name is already on your phone, the phone asks "Is this your watch?" Tap yes.
  • Swipe left and right for the four pages: Tree, Today, Play and Me.
  • In Present Catch, turn the crown to move the sack.

Common problems

  • "That name is taken." It was picked on another phone. Whoever runs the group can unlock it: menu, People, tap the name, Unlock.
  • I forgot my PIN or password. Whoever runs the group can unlock your name the same way. Then pick it again.
  • The music will not start. Music needs an internet connection. On Apple Watch it may also need headphones or a speaker connected.
  • Something is not showing up. Check the connection. The app saves changes on your device and sends them when it is back online.

Report a wish or a person

Open the wish and tap "Report or hide this wish". Whoever runs the group can take it off for everyone. You can also hide a whole list just for yourself.

If something serious is going on, email us (the address is below) and include the group's invite code. We look at reports within 24 hours.

Delete your data

Open the menu, tap Safety and rules, then Delete my stuff. To delete a whole group, contact us with its invite code.
